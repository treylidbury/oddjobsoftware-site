Load Planner privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Support Odd Job Software

Summary

Load Planner is made by Odd Job Software, the independent app studio of Trey Lidbury. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

Information the app collects

None. Load Planner has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it doesn't send your data anywhere. I can't see what you enter in the app.

Information stored on your device

The trailer sizes, products and quantities you enter are saved only on your iPhone so they're there next time you open the app. This data is included in your device backups, which are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can remove it at any time by deleting the app.

Purchases

The optional unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. I don't receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app only checks with the App Store whether the unlock has been purchased.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Emails you send me

If you email for support, I'll use your email address and message only to reply to you. I won't share them or add you to any mailing list.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a future version adds a feature that sends data, I'll update this page and the date above before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@oddjobsoftware.com
